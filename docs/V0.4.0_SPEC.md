# Hurry-Go-Round v0.4.0 — Workers & Automation Loop

## Goal

Extend the manual farm-to-market economy with two visible, separately hired automation stages while keeping manual harvesting, crate pickup, delivery, sales, cash collection, and upgrades available.

## Included

- A 16-unit field collection crate with matching pickup area, individual bundle slots, Japanese labels, and automatic one-unit player pickup.
- One harvest worker hired for 40 coins. The worker follows static field waypoints, selects ready crops every 250 ms at most, harvests for 850 ms, carries four wheat, and deposits into the crate one unit every 170 ms.
- One transport worker, unlocked after the harvest worker and hired for 75 coins. The worker loads six units at 150 ms per unit, follows a fixed crate-to-barn route at 185 units per second, and unloads at 150 ms per unit.
- Separate hiring stations with 900 ms hold progress, exact affordability feedback, one-time purchase protection, and Japanese labels.
- Explicit worker phases, persistent visible cargo, Japanese world status bubbles, a camera-independent automation HUD, and event-driven Japanese tutorial guidance.
- Pure hiring and transfer logic with invariant tests covering every worker, crate, player, barn, and market wheat location.

## Interface policy

All public gameplay labels, tutorials, statuses, zones, and purchase feedback are Japanese. The product title and `v0.4.0` remain unchanged.
