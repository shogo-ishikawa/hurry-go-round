# Hurry-Go-Round v0.2.0 — Visual Harvest Prototype

## Goal

Deliver one polished, repeatable farm loop: move to wheat, harvest automatically, carry up to 12 bundles, unload automatically at the barn, and return as crops regrow.

## Included gameplay

- A 2000 × 1400 bright 2.5D farm with two wheat plots, an oval circulation path, barn, delivery platform, pond, fences, trees, flowers, crates, barrel, sign, and layered ground detail.
- A directional female farmer assembled from original vector-like Phaser shapes, with a woven hat, flower pin, ponytail, expressive face, detailed workwear, shadow, idle breathing, walking motion, carrier basket, and attached bundle stack.
- Three simultaneous movement interfaces:
  - continuous held-direction movement with WASD or arrow keys;
  - continuous held-direction movement with an always-available on-screen joystick on PC, tablet, and mobile;
  - click/tap point movement and drag-to-retarget movement to a bounded world destination.
- Manual keyboard or joystick input takes priority and cancels active point movement immediately.
- Pointer interactions with HUD or joystick regions never create world destinations.
- A visible pulsing world marker shows the current point destination and disappears on arrival or manual cancellation.
- Bounded movement, smooth camera following, and responsive camera zoom.
- Thirty wheat nodes with ready, harvested, and growing visuals. Nearby crops harvest every 280 ms and regrow independently in 7.6–8.62 seconds.
- A 12-unit carrying limit, automatic one-at-a-time barn delivery every 180 ms, and conserved inventory.
- Synchronized physical and HUD capacity feedback:
  - an empty carrier basket remains visible before harvesting;
  - each collected wheat unit adds one visible bundle to the farmer's back;
  - all 12 physical bundles remain individually represented;
  - a red tie and flag identify the full physical stack;
  - a responsive 12-slot HUD meter fills one slot per collected unit;
  - numerical remaining capacity is always visible;
  - near-full and full states use stronger visual emphasis;
  - a persistent full banner remains until unloading starts;
  - unloading removes physical bundles and HUD slots one at a time.
- A camera-independent responsive HUD, staged non-modal tutorial, transient feedback, and one-time portrait suggestion.

## Input behavior

1. Holding WASD, an arrow key, or the virtual joystick moves continuously for as long as the input remains active.
2. The virtual joystick remains visible and usable on desktop as well as touch devices.
3. Clicking or tapping an ordinary farm location converts the screen point through the active world camera and starts automatic movement toward the bounded destination.
4. Dragging across the ordinary farm area continually replaces the destination with the current pointer position; releasing preserves the final destination.
5. Clicking, tapping, or dragging to a new location replaces the previous destination.
6. Any keyboard or joystick direction cancels point movement and receives immediate priority.
7. Arrival uses a small radius so the farmer stops cleanly without oscillation or overshoot.
8. HUD, tutorial, version, movement-hint, and joystick regions are excluded from point movement.
9. Blur, resize, orientation changes, pointer cancellation, and joystick release clear transient held input so movement cannot become stuck.

## Carry-capacity behavior

1. Logical carried inventory, the physical back stack, HUD slot count, and numerical count must always agree.
2. Capacity is sourced from the central game configuration rather than repeated literals.
3. Carried inventory is clamped between zero and the configured capacity.
4. The near-full presentation begins at 75% capacity.
5. The full state begins only when carried inventory equals capacity.
6. The full banner remains visible while full and clears after the first successful unload.
7. Compact portrait layouts use two rows of six capacity slots and move tutorial text below the inventory panel.
8. Desktop and landscape layouts use one row of twelve capacity slots.
9. The expanded HUD region is excluded from click, tap, and drag world navigation.

## Deferred

Customers, sales, currency, workers, automation, upgrades, land purchasing, saves, IndexedDB, PWA and offline support, service workers, audio, accounts, analytics, advertising, external APIs, multiple scenarios, animals, multiplayer, and barn capacity are intentionally excluded.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The field-to-barn loop works without action buttons.
2. Holding WASD, arrow keys, or the on-screen joystick produces continuous movement on PC.
3. Holding the joystick produces continuous movement on mobile and tablet.
4. Clicking or tapping a farm point moves the farmer to that point and stops without overshoot.
5. Dragging across the farm continually redirects the farmer and uses the released position as the final destination.
6. Manual movement cancels an active point destination immediately.
7. UI and joystick interactions never create accidental world destinations.
8. Crops cannot award twice and use deterministic delta-time lifecycle logic.
9. Carried inventory never exceeds 12; unloading conserves total inventory.
10. The farmer, HUD slots, and HUD count show the same carried amount at 0, 1, 9, 11, and 12 units.
11. The full state is unmistakable and remains visible until unloading begins.
12. Desktop, mobile landscape, and mobile portrait remain playable without fixed canvas dimensions.
13. The production site uses the `/hurry-go-round/` GitHub Pages base path.
