# Hurry-Go-Round v0.2.0 — Visual Harvest Prototype

## Goal

Deliver one polished, repeatable farm loop: walk to wheat, harvest automatically, carry up to 12 bundles, unload automatically at the barn, and return as crops regrow.

## Included gameplay

- A 2000 × 1400 bright 2.5D farm with two wheat plots, an oval circulation path, barn, delivery platform, pond, fences, trees, flowers, crates, barrel, sign, and layered ground detail.
- A directional female farmer assembled from original vector-like Phaser shapes, with a hat, ponytail, workwear, face, shadow, idle breathing, walking bob, and attached bundle stack.
- Keyboard movement and a dedicated touch joystick, bounded movement, smooth camera following, and responsive camera zoom.
- Thirty wheat nodes with ready, harvested, and growing visuals. Nearby crops harvest every 280 ms and regrow independently in 7.6–8.62 seconds.
- A 12-unit carrying limit, visible carried bundles, automatic one-at-a-time barn delivery every 180 ms, and conserved inventory.
- A camera-independent responsive HUD, staged non-modal tutorial, transient feedback, and one-time portrait suggestion.

## Deferred

Customers, sales, currency, workers, automation, upgrades, land purchasing, saves, IndexedDB, PWA and offline support, service workers, audio, accounts, analytics, advertising, external APIs, multiple scenarios, animals, multiplayer, and barn capacity are intentionally excluded.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The field-to-barn loop works without action buttons.
2. Crops cannot award twice and use deterministic delta-time lifecycle logic.
3. Carried inventory never exceeds 12; unloading conserves total inventory.
4. Desktop, mobile landscape, and mobile portrait remain playable without fixed canvas dimensions.
5. The production site uses the `/hurry-go-round/` GitHub Pages base path.
