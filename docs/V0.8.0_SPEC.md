# Hurry-Go-Round v0.8.0 — Operations Center & Reliable Workforce

v0.8.0 is an incremental operations update. It preserves the v0.7.0 resource, contract, input, and IndexedDB loops while making farm facilities authoritative and expanded-area hiring visible and reliable.

## Facilities and interactions

`FacilityRegistry` and `InteractionRegistry`-equivalent immutable definitions are the source of facility bounds, landmarks, Japanese labels, logical/visible radii, hold durations, and worker-role links. The carry upgrade belongs to the shared farm-management sign; production signs are resolved by the runtime sign-layout function with a 40-world-unit gap. Corn and poultry hire pads use the same 82-unit visible and logical range and 900 ms hold.

## Operations and workers

The farm operations office opens only within its marked world-space range. Its roster lists all five roles and routes hire and training actions through the same pure workforce transactions used by on-site hiring. Worker levels 1–3 affect movement speed, work interval, and cargo capacity. Corn harvesters, corn transporters, and poultry caretakers are visible world entities making one-unit, resource-conserving trips rather than invisible direct automation.

## Persistence

Save schema 2 stores explicit worker levels and operations preferences. Valid checksummed schema-1 v0.7.0 envelopes migrate to schema 2, preserving canonical progression and worker cargo, assigning level 1 to hired workers and level 0 to workers not hired, and calculating a new checksum. Computed sign placement and runtime paths remain transient. Hiring and training request priority saves.

## Compatibility

The native IndexedDB primary/backup policy, SHA-256 envelopes, JSON import/export limit, contract behavior, keyboard/touch controls, Phaser/Vite architecture, and GitHub Pages base `/hurry-go-round/` are unchanged.
