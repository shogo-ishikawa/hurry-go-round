# Hurry-Go-Round v0.3.0 — Market & First Upgrade Loop

## Goal

Extend the visual harvest prototype into its first economic progression loop: harvest wheat, deliver it, stock a market, serve customers, collect earnings, and buy visibly faster harvesting.

## Included

- The complete v0.2.0 harvesting, carrying, regrowth, and barn-delivery loop.
- An attached farm market with eight individually readable shelf slots, a till, coin pile, customer queue markings, and automatic one-unit restocking.
- Four original customer variations with entering, FIFO queueing, buying, out-of-stock waiting, and leaving phases.
- Atomic one-wheat sales for two coins, physical till accumulation, and automatic one-coin wallet collection.
- A world-space Harvest Speed pad with hold progress, affordability feedback, re-entry locking, two purchases, and three interval levels.
- WASD, arrow keys, an always-available virtual joystick, and click/tap/drag point movement with a destination marker and reserved HUD regions.
- Responsive inventory/economy HUD panels and a staged, non-modal market tutorial.

## Balance

- Market capacity: 8; restock: one unit per 220 ms.
- Customer spawn: 2,400 ms; purchase: 750 ms; queue: 4; active cap: 6.
- Wheat price: 2 coins; cash pickup: one coin per 55 ms within 95 units.
- Upgrade hold: 900 ms within 90 units.
- Harvest intervals: 280, 220, and 170 ms; upgrade costs: 20 and 55 coins.

## Invariants

Transfers conserve unsold wheat across carried, barn, and market inventory. A sale removes exactly one wheat and creates exactly the configured price in the till. Till collection conserves total coins. Upgrade purchases remove exactly the stated wallet cost. No inventory, currency, stock, or upgrade level may become negative or exceed its configured cap.

## Deferred

Workers, pathfinding, land expansion, additional crops, animals, processing, recipes, multiple stores, customization, persistence, offline progression, PWA support, service workers, audio, accounts, cloud features, rankings, achievements, advertising, payments, external APIs, multiplayer, and a generalized economy framework remain out of scope.
