# Hurry-Go-Round v0.6.0 — Mixed Cargo, Visual Polish & Expanded Automation

v0.6.0 incrementally extends the v0.5.0 world from main commit `5d9047da2cc08c338b271efa52e910ceb00f005f`. Player cargo is one authoritative resource-keyed basket with a shared capacity of 12, 18, or 24 and round-robin wheat, corn, and egg unloading. Farmer and responsive HUD presentation expose every carried resource.

Pure deterministic modules define cargo transfers, sign placement/LOD, prioritized guidance, corn worker transfers, caretaker task selection, exact-cost hiring, and front-customer stock-out patience. Patience lasts 12 seconds, warns below 50%, becomes critical below 20%, resets when stock returns, and commits FIFO abandonment without stock, coins, or successful-sale changes.

Corn automation uses a 20-unit roadside crate, five-unit harvester, and eight-unit transporter with hire costs 160 and 240 coins. The 300-coin poultry caretaker carries at most six of one resource, prioritizing emergency feed, stored eggs, then feed top-up. Public status text is Japanese. Authored routes and coordinated facility boards avoid crop, building, coop, queue, and interaction areas.

The Pages base remains `/hurry-go-round/`; Phaser primitives and local palette assets remain the only runtime presentation dependencies.
