# Hurry-Go-Round v0.9.0 — Processing Yard & Production Planning

v0.9.0 incrementally extends v0.8.0 with seven canonical resources and a processing yard. The yard costs 800 coins after the east field and coop; the grain mill costs 350 and the bakery costs 850. Central deterministic recipes convert wheat to flour, corn to cornmeal, and processed grain plus eggs into bread and cornbread.

Machines have shared-capacity input/output buffers, atomic reserved-input cycles, three levels, automatic or fixed recipe selection, and resumable active-cycle state. A mill operator and baker use role-specific cargo allowlists, batched capacities, exact hire/training costs, and authored-route runtime phases. Balanced, market-first, contract-first, and processing-first routing protect feed, contracts, market targets, reserves, and active production.

Save schema 3 preserves the schema 1 → 2 migration and migrates schema-2 resource collections to seven keys without changing existing quantities, contracts, PR #18 corn-field levels, seeds, or sequences. IndexedDB envelopes, SHA-256 checksums, controls, contract delivery, customer patience, settings, and the `/hurry-go-round/` Pages base remain compatible.
